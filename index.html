About Me

With 15 years of teaching and research experience in Computer Science, I love to learn and teach cutting edge techniques of Artificial Intelligence.

I completed my Ph.D. in Computer Science Engineering in 2014, received a Master of Technology degree in Computer and Information Technology with a Gold Medal, and also completed a Master of Science & Master of Philosophy in Physics. My areas of specialization are Data Science, Machine Learning/Deep Learning, Computer Vision, and Brain-Computer Interfaces.

I have published more than 50 research papers in reputed international journals. As part of my Ph.D., I worked on designing a computer-aided diagnosis tool to automatically detect breast cancer cells in mammograms. I have also worked on developing deep learning algorithms to identify and classify skin cancer cells and analyze brain tissue on MRI images. I have mentored many research scholars in Artificial Intelligence and Brain-Computer Interface research projects. My current research focuses on Brain-Computer Interfaces to analyze EEG signals using machine learning techniques. My research projects are funded by many internal and external sources.

I am blessed to have a beautiful family with two adorable kids and a loving husband who are the reason behind my every success, and my parents who have been the boon throughout my career. My hobbies are cooking, painting, and yoga.
